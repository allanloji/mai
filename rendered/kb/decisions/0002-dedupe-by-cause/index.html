0002 · Merge verified causes within a product

owner Allan Loji and Codex accepted reviewed 2026-11-04 · 1 days ago

Replaces: 0001 · Duplicates share a subject line. The accepted original remains unchanged as history.

Context

The review of #4471 shows that the same symptom and subject can hide different bugs. One desk handles multiple products; a similar phrase does not establish a shared cause across them.

Decision

Merge only after verifying the same product, failure mechanism, and relevant affected path. Read the body and check tenant settings, locale, channel, and form version. For the date failures, use the tenant-based classifier and the rendered examples. A different subject does not block merging if cause is established. A matching subject does not justify merging if causes differ or remain unknown.

Keep parser failures and booking-v3 hint failures in separate tickets. Preserve unique report IDs and counts; repeated export rows are not new reports. Hold unknown tenants, unsupported products, or contradictory symptoms for manual investigation. Reconcile changed classifications explicitly, without silently double counting.
