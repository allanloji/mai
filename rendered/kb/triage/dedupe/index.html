Triage: verify the cause before merging

owner Allan Loji and Codex active reviewed 2026-11-04 · 1 days ago

Follow decision 0002, which replaces subject-only matching in decision 0001. See #4471 reviewed for why the change is needed.

  1. Read the report body and identify its product, tenant and affected path.
  2. Look up tenant settings and the entry channel. For date reports, compare tenant date order, booking form version and the failure mechanism using the classifier below. Do not infer cause from browser locale or intake tag alone.
  3. Merge only into a ticket with the same verified cause and product scope. Different subjects may describe the same failure; matching subjects can hide different failures.
  4. Keep parser and booking-v3 hint reports apart. Count each report ID once and retain its evidence.
  5. If the tenant, cause or product is unknown, or symptoms conflict with classification, investigate before merging. Keep the original record and document later reclassification.
